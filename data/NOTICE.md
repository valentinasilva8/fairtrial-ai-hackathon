# Third-party content notice

`data/trialwatch_argument_excerpts.jsonl` contains short excerpts from TrialWatch fairness reports
published by the **Clooney Foundation for Justice (CFJ)** at https://cfj.org/reports/.

**© Clooney Foundation for Justice. All rights reserved by CFJ.** These excerpts are **not** covered by
this repository's MIT license.

They are reproduced, with attribution and a link to the source page for every excerpt, for a
non-commercial project built for the TrialWatch Fair Trial & AI Hackathon (Columbia Law School Human
Rights Institute, October 2026), for which the team was given access to TrialWatch materials. Only the
paragraphs the app displays are included (the strongest argument per category for each of 47 reports);
the full reports are not redistributed. To use the full reports, read them on cfj.org.

If CFJ would like any excerpt removed, delete the file and the app falls back to showing links only.

The other `data/trialwatch_*.csv` files contain metadata derived from the reports (titles, links, grades
with the short sentence they were read from, keyword counts and outcome sentences), with the same
attribution.
